WHAT THE APP WILL LOOK LIKE
───────────────────────────
- The background image covers the whole window, with no empty bars.
- The yellow header runs across the full width. The save icon sits at the
  right edge and the heading in the middle.
- The form is centered and zoomed to fill the window height, at the same
  large size as the original app.
- The heading reads "iQuery and Mavericks Log". The labels read "iQueries
  Completed" and "Mavericks Completed". Labels use Open Sans.
- The white panels behind the fields are lighter (60% instead of 85%
  opaque), so the background shows through more. The input boxes stay
  solid white.

How it works
- Scale to fit ON makes the player zoom the whole app to the window, so
  the form stays large.
- Lock aspect ratio OFF lets the canvas widen or lengthen to the window's
  shape.
- The screen takes that full size, so the background image (already set
  to ImagePosition.Fill) covers everything.
- The form keeps its original 700 width and is centered.

Why not the original layout: the original screen was only 700 x 650
inside a 1136 x 640 canvas. The background only ever covered a 700-wide
rectangle.

Why not Responsive: Responsive layout never zooms. The 700-wide form
stays small on a large monitor.


HOW TO USE THIS FILE
────────────────────
- Work through the sections in order, A to L. Each section is one control.
  Do every item of that control, then move on.
- Paste formulas in the formula bar without an initial equals sign.
- Data cards are locked. Select the card in Tree view and use Advanced >
  Unlock to change properties before you edit the card or its controls.
- Type layout values in the formula bar. Do not drag controls, or Studio
  rewrites the positions.
- QUICK CHECK: after sections A and B and item E2 (Form X):
  1. Add a temporary label to EditScreen1 with
     Text = App.Width & " x " & App.Height
  2. Press F5 with the browser maximized and note the value shown.
  3. Check that the background covers the whole window and the form is
     centered.
  4. Delete the temporary label.
  If the background does not cover the window, stop and tell me what you
  saw and the value from the label.

Not changed: the header bar height, the heading width and row, the form
width and position under the header, all card and input sizes,
IconCancel1, Icon2, Icon2_1, the ErrorMessage and "*" labels,
txtTotalCompleted, all DataField/Update/Default bindings, Form.OnSuccess
and Form.OnFailure, and the SharePoint list 'Daily PNQ Log' (columns
PNQsCompleted and WilbursCompleted keep their names).

    Lock aspect ratio off removes the empty bars at the sides.

    BEFORE
    600

TESTING IN DEV
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
1. Save in dev. Press F5 with the browser maximized. Expected: the
   background image covers the whole window with no empty bars. The
   header spans the full width, with the save icon at the right and the
   heading centered. The form is centered and fills the height below the
   header, as large as in the original app.
2. Resize the browser window (narrower, wider, shorter). Expected: the
   background still covers the window and the form stays centered. On a
   window narrower than about 16:9 (for example 1920 x 1200), the app
   zooms to fit the width. The form stays centered and empty space appears
   below the fields.
3. Text: the heading reads "iQuery and Mavericks Log" on one line. The
   labels read "iQueries Completed" and "Mavericks Completed". All labels
   and the Recovery Date picker use Open Sans. The Work Date picker and
   the Reason dropdown keep their default font. Hover the iQueries input:
   its tooltip reads "iQueries Completed".
   The panels behind the fields are lighter than before, the label text
   is still easy to read, and the input boxes are solid white.
4. Enter a total of 8 or more and press Save. Expected: "Entry submitted
   successfully." and one new row in the dev SharePoint list, with the
   counts in PNQsCompleted and WilbursCompleted.
5. Enter a total below 8, pick a non-exempt reason, and leave the recovery
   fields empty. Expected: the message mentions "iQueries and Mavericks"
   and no row is created. Fill both recovery fields and save: the row is
   created.
6. The reload icon clears the form. The database and dashboard icons open
   their links.
7. Publish to dev, open the dev player link, and repeat steps 1 and 2.
